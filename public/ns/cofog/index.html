COFOG 1999

The United Nations' Classification of the Functions of Government (COFOG) 1999 as published by PublicInformation.ie: ten divisions, split into 69 groups and 109 classes. PublicInformation.ie uses it to classify agenda-item topics in its SPC meetings dataset.

Term URIs

Every term has a persistent URI of the form https://data.publicinformation.ie/ns/cofog#<code>, for example https://data.publicinformation.ie/ns/cofog#04.5.1 ("Road transport"). This namespace means COFOG 1999; a future revision of the classification will be published under a new namespace and will not change what these URIs mean. Each code below links to its term URI.
