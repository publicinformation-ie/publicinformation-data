Home Building Finance Ireland (HBFI) has obligations under the Freedom of Information Act 2014 (the “FOI Act”), the European Communities (Access to Information on the Environment) Regulations 2007 and the Data Protection legislation, including The General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the Data Protection Acts 1988 – 2018.

In this section you will find information relating to the above legislation, including how it operates in and applies to HBFI, as well as details on how to contact the organisation should you wish to make a request pursuant to the above pieces of legislation.

This section also contains the Freedom of Information Publication Scheme, which aims to make information that is publically available easier to access by providing a designated area, comprising of different sections, where details about the organisation can be located. Within this scheme you will find a wide range of details about HBFI which may be useful to members of the public seeking to find out information about HBFI without having to make a request under the FOI Act.
