Carlow to Welcome European Visitors for Entente Florale Europe 2026

Carlow County Council is preparing to welcome delegates and representatives from across Europe as host of Entente Florale Europe 2026, taking place on 18 and 19 September 2026.
Carlow's selection as host follows its remarkable success in the prestigious European competition, where Carlow Town was awarded a Gold Medal in 2024, along with the President's Prize in recognition of its innovative Rain Gardens project. These achievements earned international recognition for Carlow's commitment to sustainability, biodiversity, environmental stewardship and community-led development.

Affordable Housing at Railway Close, Tullow, Co. Carlow

Carlow County Council is delighted to launch an ‘Affordable Housing Scheme’ at Railway Close, Shillelagh Road, Tullow Co. Carlow.
A total of 2 affordable homes consisting of 2 nr 3-bedroom semi-detached two storey dwellings are being made available for sale by Carlow County Council in the residential development of Railway Close, Tullow Co. Carlow under affordable dwelling purchase arrangements in accordance with the Affordable Housing Act 2021 and associated Affordable Housing Regulations.
