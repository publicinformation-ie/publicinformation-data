Fáilte | Welcome

Welcome to the homepage of the University's Freedom of Information (FOI) Unit.

The Freedom of Information Act 2014 provide for members of the public to gain access to records held by public bodies subject to certain conditions. The role of the FOI Unit is to provide information and support on all matters relating to the application of the Freedom of Information Act 2014 within the University and this section of the website is intended to inform and assist staff, students and any other interested parties in understanding how the University performs that role.  

The FOI Unit comes under the Office of the Chief Operations Officer for admin and management purposes.

Robbie Roulston - Deputy COO & Freedom of Information Officer
