## **Master Policy Inventory**

The following table lists the authoritative climate policies in the Republic of Ireland that the document pipeline ingests. Annual Climate Action Plans are editions of a single standing policy, so one row covers every plan year (CAP23, CAP24, CAP25, ...) and the progress reports published against them.

| Policy / Strategy Title | Primary Issuing Authority | Current Policy Horizon | Scope & Strategic Pillar | Core Statutory & Framework Linkages |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Climate Action Plan** | Department of Climate, Energy and the Environment (DCEE) | Annual statutory update to 2030 carbon budgets and 2050 climate neutrality | Economy-Wide Roadmap of Actions for Carbon Budgets & Sectoral Emissions Ceilings | Climate Action and Low Carbon Development (Amendment) Act 2021, Carbon Budgets, Sectoral Emissions Ceilings |
