## 1. Fix before relying on the regenerated bundles

### 1.1 `assemble_sections`: slug collision duplicates a section — FIXED (2026-11-22)
Fixed on branch `fix/assemble-sections-slug-collision`. `build()` now de-duplicates the `chapter/slug` composite key per boundary: the chapter keeps the reserved `overview` slug for its preamble, a colliding node is suffixed `-2`, `-3`… (the `dedupe_slugs` convention), and a `SectionSlugCollision` error is logged. Regression test: `tests/test_assemble_sections.py::test_a_node_slugged_overview_gets_a_suffixed_slug_rather_than_sharing_the_preamble_file`. The national-cycle bundle regenerated accordingly: preamble `overview` pp. 63–64, the real node `overview-2` pp. 198–500 (129,534 words once), `full.md` −143 KB, and a re-scan of all 40 bundles finds no duplicate section file anywhere.

### 1.2 `national-cycle-network-plan`: one chapter spans pages 63 to 1107 — RESOLVED (2026-11-22, human decision)
The back matter is not appendices of the Plan but **two separately-authored statutory reports bound into the same PDF**: the SEA Environmental Report (Updated) behind the Plan's `Appendix G` divider (pp. 193–493, containing the `Appendix B: Corridor option assessment tables` at pp. 302–448) and the Appropriate Assessment Screening and Natura Impact Statement (pp. 494–1107, Appendices A–D at pp. 567–1100). The human chose to **split them into their own chapters**. The gold grows 8 → 10 chapters with new rulebook rule **R1a** (a bound-in report is a chapter of the bundle); the detector stays TOC-faithful by R1, and `detect_structure/override.json` promotes the two supplemented nodes (`overview` → "SEA Environmental Report (Updated)" at p. 193; `2-methodology` → "Appropriate Assessment Screening and Natura Impact Statement" at p. 494, `number` nulled). Measurement in RESULTS.md 1.2: the published tree is unchanged everywhere (tuning 0.81 / held-out 0.62 / all 0.75; this document 10/10 fully correct post-override); only its raw tree drops (exact F1 1.00 → 0.89, page attribution 1.00 → 0.17), by design and explained. Bundle regenerated: MELF is pp. 63–192 with its four real sections; the two reports are their own chapters. Teaching the detector to find bound-in reports generally (option C) was considered and **deferred**: this PDF has no outline to lean on, so a general rule would guess at report title pages, and 6 documents carry `Appendix` headings that would each need gold review first (RESULTS.md 1.2 notes).

The bundle rebuilds `publishable: true` with p. 102 declared furniture. Still held pending the human hold-lift review.

## 4. The D4 section-loss trade-off (decide)
`SupplementHeadingRejected` removes numbered sections from six published trees (CAP 2023 213 to 158 nodes, emra 83 to 53, national-cycle, ev-infrastructure, CAP 2025 annex, fishing). Most of the rejected headings in the first four are real sections, rejected because the chapter that owns them was not matched in the right place. The repo rule (an explicit error beats a confidently wrong value) was applied. The alternative is to keep any heading whose number matches some TOC chapter and accept that some sit under the wrong chapter. Revisit once the chapter-matching problems above are fixed, since that recovers most of the sections.
