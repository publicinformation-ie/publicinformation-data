We Love Markets Monthly Flea Market

We Love Markets is a monthly flea market that takes place on The Digital Hub campus on the last Sunday of every month. The Market hosts up to 50 eclectic stalls, showcasing a mixture of local design and locally made artisan food, homemade crafts, clothes, furniture, bric-a-brac, vintage wares, artisan goods and plenty of hidden gems. The market supports local enterprises, organisations, designers and creatives in Dublin 8. The next market will be taking place on Sunday, 28th June from 11am - 4pm at the Digital Depot car park.

📣 Two days left to apply - Call for Expressions of Interest in leasing The Bank at The Digital Hub 📣

The Digital Hub Development Agency (DHDA) is inviting expressions of interest from experienced individuals, organisations and consortia interested in leasing The Bank at The Digital Hub through a public tender process.

The opportunity is open to those whose proposed use of the building aligns with The Digital Hub’s vision, mission and strategic objectives.

📅 Register your interest by 6pm on Friday, 5th June 2026.

DHDA is exploring the potential for an organisation or individual to take on an initial three-year lease of the building and deliver a vibrant programme of public activities from the space.

An information booklet is available, providing details on the building’s history, ownership, current use, dimensions and features, as well as the proposed tenant profile and the public tender process.

Learn more via the LinkTree in our bio.

The Digital Hub — Dublin, Ireland

A place for hybrid working, playing, innovating, welcoming, meeting, connecting, discovering, brainstorming, questioning, learning, supporting, collaborating, thinking, prototyping, visualising, building, testing, inspiring, and scaling and growing.

About Us

Space to Grow

Our campus has everything you need to focus on doing your best work — flexible office space, workspaces and meeting rooms — located in the heart of Dublin City.

A Creative Community

Be part of a curated and thriving community of startups, global companies, big thinkers, skilled technologists and creatives — all at the forefront of technology, digital media and art.

A Home for Events

Meet like-minded people, share knowledge and learn from experts and industry leaders at regular tech industry events and more.

The Digital Hub is located in the historic Liberties area of Dublin city centre.
