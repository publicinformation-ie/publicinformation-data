Get the Data

Downloadable spreadsheet files, one per dataset. Every file is a plain CSV you can open in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers — no software beyond a spreadsheet program is needed.

Public bodies

Every public body in Ireland: its name, whether it handles Freedom of Information (FOI) requests, and its FOI contact email.

ColumnWhat it means
nameThe public body's name
foi_subjectWhether this body handles FOI requests
foi_email_emailTheir FOI contact email address

FOI disclosures

Records of information already released under FOI: what was asked, who asked (e.g. journalist, member of the public), when, and the outcome.

For one public body at a time, use the per-body files listed in the manifest. Cloning the repository? The combined files are built, not committed: run python scripts/build_foi_combined.py first. Dated, immutable copies with checksums: https://releases.publicinformation.ie/foi-disclosures/.

FOI request files

The original source documents each disclosure record came from.

Who does what

Links from each public body to its page on the Government's "Who Does What" directory.

data.gov.ie links

Links from each public body to its organisation page on data.gov.ie, Ireland's open data portal.

datacatalogue.gov.ie links

Links from each public body to its organisation page on datacatalogue.gov.ie, the Public Service Data Catalogue where public sector bodies list the data they hold, including "protected data" holdings under the EU Data Governance Act. A different catalogue from data.gov.ie, and a separate dataset.

lobbying.ie links

Links from each public body to its page on lobbying.ie, Ireland's Register of Lobbying, with a point-in-time count of lobbying returns filed against it.

Staff numbers

Full-time-equivalent (FTE) staff numbers for the latest reported quarter, from the Department of Public Expenditure's public service numbers file. Each row is linked to a public body where its name could be matched; aggregates such as Second Level Education are kept as unmatched rows so the totals still add up.

Public body actions

The published commitments ("actions") of public bodies, taken from their strategy and action-plan documents, together with how each action's reported status changed in later progress reports.

ColumnWhat it means
action_textThe action as published
plan_titleThe plan that declared the action
original_deadline_startThe plan's deadline, as a normalised date
leadThe organisation leading the action

This dataset has four tables: actions (one row per published action), status observations (one row per progress report's update on an action), and relationships (how actions relate to one another), and milestones (one row per dated interim milestone of an action).

Motions

Motions moved at meetings of Irish local authorities, from the authorities' published meeting minutes: who proposed and seconded each motion, the meeting date and type, and the outcome.

SPC meetings

Strategic Policy Committee meetings of three county councils, February 2025 to May 2026: Meath County Council (Transportation and Urban Regeneration), Kildare County Council (Transport, Mobility and Open Spaces) and Westmeath County Council (Planning, Regeneration & Transportation): who attended, their attendance, agenda items with COFOG 1999 topics, official decisions as verbatim quotes, and the minutes themselves rendered verbatim as Markdown. Five tables — meetings, people, attendance, items and decisions — join on meeting_id and person_id. Topic codes come from the COFOG 1999 vocabulary (JSON, CSV and SKOS Turtle), and transport items carry transport-activity groupings. The earlier transport-subdomain tags are retired and are mapped to those coarser groupings.

Active Travel allocations

Every project the National Transport Authority gave Active Travel Investment Grant money to between 2022 and 2026: which local authority received it, the project's code and name, how much was allocated, and which page of which PDF it came from. Each row is one grant allocation for one project in one year, so the same project appears once for every year it was funded — filter the project_code column to follow a scheme across the five years.

Transport RACI

Who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed for local transport activities and lifecycle stages. This is analysis compiled by PublicInformation.ie, not official data: the assignments have been analysed for Meath County Council only. Each row is one activity × lifecycle stage.

FileWhat it holds
assignments.csvOne line per assessed assignment, with activity, stage, evidence and public note.
assignment-roles.csvOne line per role (R, A, C or I) and party.
assignment-sources.csvThe source IDs behind each assignment.
rows.csvEvery activity × stage row and whether it has been assessed.
raci-wide.csvThe readable view: one line per assignment with party names and evidence in plain words.
variants.csvThe variant labels used on assignments.
sources.csvThe citation and URL for every source.
boundary-rules.csvThe rules that decide which stage a decision belongs to.

Read the README for how to read a RACI line and which roles are still awaiting review, or take the nested JSON.

Worked example: find a body's FOI contact email

  1. Open public-bodies.csv in a spreadsheet program.
  2. Search or filter the name column for the body you're interested in.
  3. Read the foi_email_email column on that row — that's their FOI contact address.
