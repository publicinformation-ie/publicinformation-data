## Agenda items

## Item 1: Minutes {#item-1}

On the proposal of Cllr Farrell and seconded by Cllr Gaffney, the minutes of the SPC meeting held on the 19 September were approved.

## Item 2: Matters Arising {#item-2}

Jean Ryan, Director of Service, responded to a question regarding the proposed Westmeath County Council Taking in Charge Policy and green space ownership in private developments, confirming the policy has been updated including a section on open spaces. It was also noted that the County Development Plan and Local Area Plans identify land zoning across the county. The Westmeath County Council Taking in Charge Policy is included on the agenda for the full Council meeting in December 2025.

## Item 3: To receive a presentation from Damien O’Neill TFI on the Local Link route {#item-3}

proposals in Westmeath Damien O’Neill, TFl, provided a comprehensive presentation on the local link route proposals that service Co. Westmeath. Members welcomed the update and supplementary questions in relation to route expansion options, additional services, funding \& resources and proposed plans for additional and illuminated bus stops, were responded to.

## Item 4: To receive an update on the Winter Readiness Programme 2025-2026. {#item-4}

Damien Grennan, Senior Engineer provided an update on Westmeath County Councils Winter Readiness programme for 2025-2026. Comprehensive statistics and operation plans for extreme weather incidents were presented to the members. Members welcomed the update and noted the positive work and community assistance carried out by the Transport team in Westmeath County Council.

## Item 5: To receive an update on Regeneration Projects {#item-5}

Anne Marie Corroon, Senior Executive Officer provided a detailed presentation on the completed, ongoing and proposed future URDF, RRDF and EU Just Transition funded Regeneration Projects across the County. Members welcomed the update and supplementary questions in relation to proposed works at the Court House project in Moate, update on Delvin Bank project, timeframes for Athlone westside plans and Living in City initiative for Athlone, were responded to.

## Item 6: To discuss items for 2026 work programme. {#item-6}

## Item 7: To agree the Schedule of Meeting Dates for 2026. {#item-7}

On the proposal of Clir Gaffney and seconded by Clir Farrell, the schedule of meeting dates for 2026, were approved. Members agreed that one of the meetings in 2026 should take place in the new Kinnegad Library. It was also agreed that the first meeting will take place in the Executive Lounge in Mullingar and that all meetings will have the Hybrid option.
