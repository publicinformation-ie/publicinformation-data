Transport subdomains (retired)

This vocabulary is retired. The 17 terms below were used to tag SPC agenda items; they are replaced by transport-activity groupings, and the SPC meetings dataset publishes groupings instead of subdomains. The CSV stays published unchanged so an existing link keeps working.
