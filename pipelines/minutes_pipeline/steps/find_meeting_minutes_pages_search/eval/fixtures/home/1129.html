Our sports, recreation, and healthy living services help people stay active, connect with others, and enjoy a healthier lifestyle.

Tourism development

Find out about tourism development and promotion and our vision for Clare to be a globally-renowned sustainable and vibrant destination.

Towns - enhancement, regeneration and plans

We are implementing town regeneration plans under the Town Centre First Policy, including projects for Ennis, Shannon, and Killaloe. These initiatives focus on reducing vacancy, improving public spaces, and creating sustainable development.

Heritage and biodiversity

County Clare's heritage tells the story of how people and the environment have changed over time and its biodiversity shows the variety of plants and animals that make the area special.

Rural and community development

Integration

We support people seeking international protection, refugees, and others as they settle into Irish life, helping them connect with local services and become active members of their new communities.
