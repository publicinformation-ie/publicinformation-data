Freedom of Information

The Health Products Regulatory Authority (HPRA) is committed to transparency, openness, and accountability in providing information to the public. As a public body, we adhere to the Freedom of Information (FOI) Act 2014. For further information, click the links below. 

Submitting an FOI request

Further information FOI in the HPRA can be found here.

Learn more
FOI publication scheme

HPRA is required to publish as much information as possible in a routine, open, and accessible way, in line with the principles of openness, transparency, and accountability.

Learn more
Disclosure logs

The Authority has a policy of posting a disclosure summary of non-personal FOI requests on its website for reference purposes.
