Freedom of Information (FOI)

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2014, anyone is entitled to apply to An Coimisiún Pleanála for access to information that is held by it and not otherwise publicly available.

Applications for information under the Freedom of Information Act should be addressed to:

FOI Officer,
An Coimisiún Pleanála
64 Marlborough Street,
Dublin 1.

Phone: (01) 858 8100
Email: foi@pleanala.ie

For further details of how to get information from An Coimisiún Pleanála under the Freedom of Information Act 2014:

  • How to get information under the Freedom of Information Act
