## Agenda items

## Item 1: Minutes {#item-1}

On the proposal of Cllr Farrell and seconded by Alison Hough the minutes of the SPC meeting held on the 17 May 2024 were approved.

## item 2: Matters Arising. {#item-2}

Alison Hough advised the meeting that the motion “right to nature” which was endorsed by the SPC on the 17 May 2024 was moved as a Motion by the SPC Chair Cllr. Heavin and approved at the full May Council meeting held on the 27 May 2024. Therese McCullough-Melia enquired as to whether the report on the removal of public bus transport services from Athlone had been obtained and if so, could it be circulated to the committee. Jean Ryan, Director of Service advised that she will look into the request and present an update at next SPC meeting. Cllr. Jones requested an update on the audit report for the reopening of Killucan Railway Station and could the date of the meeting schedules with the NTA be circulated. Jean Ryan, Director of Service advised that we have an agenda item on the SPC — workplan, where this can be looked at.

## Item 3: SPC Standing Orders and Terms of Reference {#item-3}

Jean Ryan, Director of Service provided a presentation on the statutory function of the SPC and asked the Committee to review the issued documents.

## Item 4: Overview of SPC Sections and Policy {#item-4}

Planning Cathaldus Hartin, Senior Planner, presented an operational overview of the Planning Department activities including an update on the progress being made on the Draft Mullingar Area Plan and Joint Athlone Area Plan. The committee welcomed the presentation and supplementary questions regarding public consultation were responded to. The Committee requested that the presentation be circulated post meeting. Regeneration Anne Marie Corroon, Senior Executive Officer, presented an overview of the extensive Regeneration team projects that are currently progressing and advised on the funding process through the various schemes. The members welcomed the presentation and supplementary questions on operational queries from the Committee in relation to specific projects were responded to. Jean Ryan, Director of Service advised that it would be possible to put together a brief of the various avenues and funding options available and suggested that this could be included as an item to the SPC Work Programme and added as a future agenda item. Transportation Damien Grennan, Senior Engineer, presented an operational overview of the Transportation \& Active Travel Department. The members welcomed the presentation, and supplementary questions were responded to including an update on the details of the LIS and CIS which the committee were advised remain open for applications and approved through the program of works. Paul Bruton asked for an update on the N4 Mullingar to Longford Roosky emerging route corridor and in reply was advised that this project fell under the remit of the NRO Office and therefore he had no details to hand. Jean Ryan advised that further public consultation is taking place on this route selection process and WCC staff will be briefing the members in the next couple of weeks. Work Programme 2025-2029 Jean Ryan, Director of Service, presented a suggested work programme for the lifetime of the SPC and opened it up to the committee for comment. On the proposal of Cllr Tom Farrell, seconded by Cllr Liam McDaniel a Roadside Trees Policy to include tree maintenance and conservation to be included in the programme. Tree Policy/Tree Maintenance/Tree Conservation and implementation of pesticide policy was raised and discussed. The question of emergency response/storm preparedness was also discussed. The Committee was advised that the Environment SPC was considering a tree management policy and that they can be invited to present to this SPC. An annual update on compliance with pesticide policy to be provided. Cllr Jones requested that the following be added to the work programme. 1. Reopening of Killucan Railway Station 2. Review of sightlines due to reduced national speed limits 3. Update on bus services in rural towns and bus services between Mullingar and Athlone

Agreed that minutes and documentation be circulated no later than a month prior to the next meeting.
