Stewarts Care is committed to the principals of openness and transparency. We routinely publish information on our website that is of interest to stakeholders and the public. This section is intended to provide you with information and guidance on exercising your rights under the Freedom of Information Act 2014. The Freedom of Information Act 2014 gives you a legal right to:

  • Access official records held by Stewarts Care

  • Have information about you changed where it is inaccurate, incomplete or misleading

  • Know the reasons for decisions made by Stewarts Care that affect you

Publication Scheme

Section 8 of the Freedom of Information Act 2014, requires Stewarts Care Ltd. to publish as much information as possible in an open and accessible manner on a routine basis outside of FOI. We are committed to making information available as part of our normal business activities. This allows for the publication of records outside of FOI provided that such publication is not prohibited by law. Our Publication Scheme is shown below. Each link leads to a page from which you can obtain relevant information in that category. If the information you require cannot be found here, you may wish to search our website, or to contact us on foi@stewartscare.ie.

How to make a request

A Freedom of Information request must be in writing – either email or letter. You can email to foi@stewartscare.ie or by post to:

Claire Carty, Freedom of Information Officer, Stewarts Care Ltd., Mill Lane, Palmerstown, Dublin 20, D20 XT80

For more information on how to make a Freedom of Information Request, you can download our Guide to making a Freedom of Information Request.

You can also use our Freedom of Information Request Form to submit a request

Click here to see a flow chart outlining the FOI Request Procedure.
