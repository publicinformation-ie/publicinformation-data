At publicjobs, we have transitioned to a new recruitment platform with the aim of enhancing our services and the candidate experience. We are working with recruitment software company, Oleeo, who are the provider of our new recruitment platform.

You can expect to see changes to some of our services, including the application process. The new process will give you more visibility and control of your application. We would appreciate your understanding and cooperation with these changes, and we will endeavour to keep you updated with relevant information.

Login here, if you already have an account on the new platform. Your username will be the email address associated with your account, and your username and password are case sensitive. If you do not already have an account on the new platform, please create one using the "Create New Account" or "Register” options.

We encourage you to set up job alerts in your account, in order to be notified about roles and suitable opportunities.

Should you have any queries, you can contact us here.

Please note that your username and password are case sensitive.
