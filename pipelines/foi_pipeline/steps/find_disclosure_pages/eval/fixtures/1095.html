Freedom of Information Act Information

Members of the public may make requests for inspection or for copies of the office’s public records under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act (“FOIA”), 5 ILCS 140/1 et seq. This Act provides that some records are exempt from inspection and copying.

Public records may include law enforcement records; court records, civil and criminal case files, audio, video, and electronic records; investigative and forensic materials; medical examiner records; financial, contract, and grant documents; subpoenas; administrative and personnel records; and child support–related records.

To make a request for inspection or for copies of office records, please make a written request via the CCSAO Records Portal, email, or postal mail. It is not necessary to submit a form or to attach a PDF. Our preference is that requests are submitted directly through the CCSAO Records Portal, where you receive confirmation of submission and retrieve responsive records.

While the SAO will make every attempt to respond to FOIA requests, we may be unable to respond within the statutory time and appreciate your cooperation; a delayed request is not a denial.

To submit a FOIA Request online with automated-receipt confirmation, please click here.

Emailed FOIA requests with no receipt confirmation may be sent to the email address SAO.FOIA[at]cookcountysao[dot]org as maintained by FOIA Officers C. Callaway, N. Anderson, J. Grace, J. Becker, and T. Bryant.

Pursuant to 5 ILCS 140 (3)(h), requests for in person inspection of records is by appointment only during regular business hours at the Cook County State's Attorney's Office at 2650 S. California Ave, Chicago, IL. Requesters must make an appointment with the FOIA officer, and an employee will be present during the inspection.

Cook County State’s Attorney’s Office Reminders on FOIA Requests:

  1. All requests must be made in writing and should specifically state which public records are desired for inspection and/or copying. The written request should include the requester’s full name and/or organization, address, telephone number, and email address to facilitate communication, discussion regarding the specific information requested, and timely responses.

  2. No request will be considered “received” by the office until it is physically received by the FOIA Officer.

  3. If a person requests a copy of a record maintained in an electronic format, the office will furnish the record in the electronic format specified by the requester, if feasible.  If it is not feasible to furnish the record in the specified electronic format, then the office will furnish it in the format in which it is maintained.
