The Freedom of Information Act 2014 asserts the right of members of the public to obtain access to official information to the greatest extent possible consistent with the public interest and the right to privacy of individuals.

The FOI Act obliges us to publish certain information on our activities and make other information available to citizens on request. Here you can access the information we publish to fulfil this obligation and a log of the non-personal FOI requests we have received. If the information you want is not already published here, you can make an FOI request using our FOI application form.

Make an FOI request

Want to submit an FOI request? Read our guide and use the FOI application form. If you've any queries, email us at foi@oireachtas.ie

Procurement policy

Purchase orders over €20,000

The Public Service Reform Plan 2011 set out that purchase orders for €20,000 or over by a Department or agency should be published online.

These data are published quarterly in arrears by the Houses of the Oireachtas Service.

2026

  • Find and download the data

Further information

  • Payments are inclusive of VAT where appropriate
  • Suppliers subject to withholding tax will have it deducted at point of payment which may decrease the amount actually paid to under €20,000
  • Penalty interest may be added at point of payments over 30 days (or whatever is agreed with the supplier), which will increase the payment
  • In addition, if the penalty interest amount calculated goes over €125 it is then subject to DIRT
  • The report includes payments for goods or services and does not include grants, grants-in-aid, reimbursements etc.
  • Some payments may be excluded from the above data if their publication would be precluded under Freedom of Information legislation
  • Payment amounts shown may relate to work completed in previous quarters or years
