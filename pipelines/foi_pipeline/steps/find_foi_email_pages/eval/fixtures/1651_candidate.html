Find Us

 By Bus


If you are travelling to Dublin by bus, BusÁras is located within walking distance of our offices (approximately 25 minute journey time).

The Luas Red Line BusÁras Stop is located outside the bus terminus which can be taken to the Jervis Stop.

For more information on bus routes to the city centre, visit Dublin Bus.

 By Train


If you are coming from Heuston Station, you can travel to our office by:

  • The Luas Red Line to the Jervis Stop (approximately 12 minutes journey time).
  • Dublin Bus to Ormond Quay and walk to Abbey Street (approximately 16 minutes journey time). For more information, see Dublin Bus.
  • Or walk (approximately 35 minutes journey time).

If you are coming from Connolly Station, you can travel to our office by:

  • The Luas Red Line to the Jervis Stop (approximately 10 minutes journey time).
  • Connolly Station is approximately 25 minute walk to our offices.

For more information, see Irish Rail.

 By Luas


Our offices are located very close to the Jervis Stop on the Luas Red Line. Please see Luas information. 

 By Car


If you are driving to Chapter House, there is parking nearby at the Jervis Centre in Dublin City Centre. Please be aware that parking is not always available.

Complaints Process

Our complaints process is outlined in our Quality Customer Action Plan which can be found on our website at ‘Customer Charter’ in the footer menu.
