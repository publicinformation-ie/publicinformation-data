Centre for Excellence in Universal Design - Údarás Náisiúnta Míchumais — National Disability Authority

The Centre for Excellence in Universal Design (CEUD), part of the National Disability Authority, is dedicated to enabling the design of environments that can be accessed, understood and used regardless of a person's age, size, ability or disability.
