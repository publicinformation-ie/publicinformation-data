    Freedom of Information

    Welcome to the UCD Freedom of Information Web Site.  Here you will find information on accessing records, information about the college and how to make a request.

    If you have any queries or feedback relating to our web site, please contact us.

    Functions

    The functions of the unit are to ensure organisational compliance with the university’s obligations under the Freedom of Information Act (FOI) 2014 and the Access to Information on the Environment Regulations 2007 - 2018. Its role is also to advise the university in the management of university records in keeping with best practice. Provision of information, assistance, and training to staff is also a responsibility of the Unit.
