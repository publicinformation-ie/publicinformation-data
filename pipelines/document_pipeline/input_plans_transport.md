## Items requiring further verification

These are the main gaps I would leave explicitly flagged in the inventory rather than treating the links above as proof of currency or exact document identity.

- National Cycle Network Plan: The linked TII page is a general strategic-planning publications page. I have not verified a direct publication page for the specific plan named in your file.

- Maritime Safety Strategy: The linked page identifies the responsible division, not a verified standalone strategy document.

- Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Strategy: The original strategy covers 2022–2025, and the Department says it is being revised for 2026–2028. Record the original and the successor separately if the revised strategy has been formally published.&#x20;
