                                  Making an Application

                                  To make a request for information via email, please forward your request to:

                                  FOI Officer
                                  Email: foi@mayococo.ie
                                  Phone: 0949064000

                                  To submit a request via post, please address requests to:

                                  FOI Officer, Corporate Development
                                  Áras an Chontae
                                  The Mall
                                  Castlebar
                                  Co. Mayo
                                  F23 WF90

                                  Our offices are open between 9.30 am and 1.00 pm and 2.00 pm and 4.30 pm, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.    

                                  You must make your request in writing, clearly indicating that the information you seek is sought under the Freedom of Information Act 2014.    

                                  If you wish to receive the information in a specific format e.g. photocopy, email, etc., you should indicate this in your request.  

                                  Please give as much detail as possible to enable us to identify the records to which you require access. Please include a daytime telephone number with your request so that you can be contacted if we have any follow-up queries. 

                                  If you have difficulty in identifying the precise records which you require, we are happy to assist you in preparing your request.   

                                  We will issue an acknowledgement of the request within two weeks of receipt. 

                                  A decision on the request will be made within four weeks of receipt, unless the request relates to such number of records that compliance within the specified time is not reasonably possible, in which case a time extension, not exceeding a further four weeks, may be allowed. You must be notified of the period of the time extension required and the reason therefore.
