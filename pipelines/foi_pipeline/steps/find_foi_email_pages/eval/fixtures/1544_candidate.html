NAMA Data Protection Statement

Executive Summary

NAMA acts as a Data Controller of Personal Data for a range of statutory purposes, including its objectives, purposes and functions under the National Asset Management Agency Act 2009.

NAMA and its group entities are committed to complying with our obligations in respect of the processing of Personal Data under data protection laws. The purpose of this Data Protection Statement ("Statement") is to ensure that we meet our transparency obligations pursuant to the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"). The Statement sets out information about our duties and responsibilities regarding the protection of Personal Data. Please click on any of the links below to access aspects of the Statement that are of particular interest:

  • Review the full Data Protection Statement.
  • Learn more about NAMA.
  • Read an explanation about how we use Personal Data.
  • Read about how to exercise any of your rights in relation to your Personal Data, such as the rights of access, erasure, rectification, objection and complaint.
  • Read a glossary of data protection terms used in the Statement.

This Statement has been adopted by NAMA with effect from 25 May 2018 which is the commencement date for the GDPR and will be reviewed from time to time. We will always post the most up to date version on the NAMA website.
