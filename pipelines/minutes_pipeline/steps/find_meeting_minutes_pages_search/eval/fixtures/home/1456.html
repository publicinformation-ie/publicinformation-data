Our Vision
Kildare County Council

Creating a sustainable future while improving quality of life for all the people of Kildare.

Climate Action, Resilience &
Environmental Protection

To show leadership in the transition to a climate resilient, biodiversity rich,
environmentally sustainable and climate neutral local economy.

Increase Energy Efficiency,
Decarbonise Energy Systems & Sources

To lead and support high standards of energy management practices and
maintain this across the organisation to ensure the 2030 energy efficiency target is met.

Housing Solutions & Supports

Address housing provision by providing housing services and supports and implementing Kildare's Housing Delivery Action Plan.

Enabling & Delivering
Sustainable Infrastructure
& Development

To enable and deliver sustainable and accessible economic, social, and physical development,
to meet the needs of the County's growing and diverse population.

Economy & Employment

To enable an inclusive, economically vibrant County that attracts the resources
and investment necessary for continued sustainable growth.

Town & Village Regeneration

Revitalising the economic and social activity of our towns and villages.

Community Leadership,Inclusion & Well-Being

Build resilient communities, supporting everyone to live full, healthy and inclusive lives and to enjoy a high quality of life at all stages.

People, Service, Resources & Innovation

Continue to implement the Local Government Reform Programme, supporting communications and service delivery by
maximising the impact of our people, funding, services and ICT resources.
