## 4. Deviations and decisions to sign off

1. **`public/index.html` has no `#reference` list.** It is a 12-line redirect
   stub to the data hub. The discovery links went to `GET_THE_DATA.md`,
   `README.md` and `get-the-data.html` instead, as recorded in the change
   handoff. No link was added to `index.html`.
2. **CSVW columns are typed** (`uri`, `integer`, `date`, `boolean`, `string`)
   with descriptions, following the motions and active-travel datasets, rather
   than all-string. Booleans are written `true`/`false`.
3. **Sweep metrics are span-tolerant.** Gold and model quotes frequently differ
   only in where the quote starts or ends, so matching allows containment with a
   length/token guard; topics score both strict primary and hierarchy-tolerant
   agreement. Method and caveats are in `REPORT.md`.
4. **`item_key` is the join key**, not `item_number`: May 2026 prints two items
   numbered `6.0` (`item-6-0`, `item-6-0-2`).
5. **One attendance row per person per meeting.** Officials who also presented
   were printed in two blocks and initially produced two rows sharing one
   `@id`, which broke both the published-ID rule and per-person counting. The
   resolver now merges them (earliest category, roles joined with `"; "`) and
   adds `presented: true`. Row count fell from 101 to 98.
6. **Extraction passes the meeting through.** `extract_spc_items` now carries
   the rendered minutes, items and attendance into its output; before the first
   real run it dropped them, and export published nothing. Covered by a
   regression test.

## 5. Residual risks

- Six meetings and one hand-labelled gold set: the model ranking is indicative,
  not statistically settled. Topic labels for procedural items are a judgment
  call, and some genuine agreements are absent from the gold set (precision
  understates the models).
- The extraction prompt is `spc-items-v1`. Any prompt change must bump the
  version so the cache invalidates.
- Adding a committee is a configuration edit, but new layout quirks may need
  parser changes; the parser fails closed on an unrecognised label or a word
  mismatch rather than guessing.
