## Agenda items

Cllr. Frankie Keena opened the meeting and welcomed the opportunity to host this SPC meeting in TUS, Athlone and highlighted that it is beneficial from economic \& educational point of view and notes that he is very proud of what TUS offers to the county. He thanked Dr. Don Faller, Dean of Faculty, Science and Health for this opportunity. Alison Hough gave brief background to TUS and expressed thanks for hosting SPC in the new science Mary Ward building.

## item 1: Minutes {#item-1}

On the proposal of Clir Farrell and seconded by James Bourke, the minutes of the SPC meeting held on the 23 May 2025 were approved.

## Item 2: Matters Arising {#item-2}

Alison Hough advised she is waiting on feedback from the PPN in relation to Trees \& Hedgerow Policy and asked that the matter be postponed until the next SPC meeting.

## Item 3: Update on Athlone Joint Urban Area Plan {#item-3}

Orla McGann, Senior Executive Planner provided a comprehensive presentation on the progress of preparation of the Athlone Joint Urban Area Plan. It is envisaged that the Draft Plan will be circulated to the Members for their consideration in December for their consideration before commencement of any formal public consultation process. The Planning Authority are engaging with the Department in terms of the implementation of the Planning and Development Act 2025 in terms of implications for the review process. Members welcomed the update and supplementary questions in relation to flood risk management, environmental assessment, availability of studies, NPF housing allocations, sport and recreation and healthcare facilities, in the context of the review, were responded to.

## Item 3: Vacancy and Dereliction {#item-3-2}

Marie McGovern Administrative Officer provided a comprehensive presentation on the priorities of Vacancy and Dereliction and outlined the Compulsory Purchase Order activation project. Members welcomed the presentation, and supplementary questions were responded to. In response to a request Marie McGovern indicated that public information sessions on this topic could be available in the future. The issue was opened to the SPC and the importance of extending funding to rural areas was highlighted as well as the need for decarbonisation of housing stock. Greater co-ordination between derelict sites under the Derelict Sites Act and derelict house under the Vacant Property Grant was also raised. Details provided in respect of planning permission exemption available (A10 Notifications) for converting vacant commercial space into residential use, subject to criteria. Anne Marie Corroon, Senior Executive Officer provided brief overview on URDF, RRDF and CPO process.

## Item 5: Update on Prefeasibility Study on Public Transport Needs for areas of Killucan, Kinnegad {#item-5}

and Hill of Down. Cllr David Jones provided update on Prefeasibility Study on Public Transport Needs for areas of Killucan, Kinnegad and Hill of Down. SPC committee endorsed the intention to discuss at next Council meeting on Monday 224 September.

## Item 6: To provide an update on the proposed Taking in Charge Policy. {#item-6}

Eugene Duffey, Senior Executive Technician provided a comprehensive presentation on the proposed Taking in Charge Policy. Members welcomed the update and supplementary questions were responded to including an update on the actions taken by WCC to engage with Developers in the early stages of developments and planning enforcement options for cases of non-compliance. On the proposal of Cllr Farrell, seconded by Clir McDaniel, it was agreed to bring the Draft Taking in Charge policy to CPG with a view to being placed on the agenda for the Council meeting on 20°" October 2025.

## Item 7: Aon Gno Eile {#item-7}

Cllr Farrell reminded the committee of previous conversation regarding Trees and Hedgerows Policy and a grant scheme policy and gave brief overview of how a Tree and Hedgerow grant scheme currently operates in Leitrim County Council. The importance of tree cutting for road safety purposes was highlighted and a request for the item to be included on the agenda for next SPC meeting on 14° November 2025 was sought. Cllr Jones requested review of sight line requirements given reduction in local road speed limits from 80km/h to 60km/h. Cathaldus Hartin Senior Planner advised that the consideration of sight line requirements will form part of next County Development Plan review. It was advised that Planning Authorities are guided by national policy from NTA and TII. Cllr Keean enquired about Integrated Wetlands and highlighted that it could be a potential policy development to serve as a wastewater treatment solution for small settlements lacking sewer infrastructure. Cathaldus Hartin advised that waste water treatment solutions for smaller settlements are guided by EPA policy in this regard. The review of the County Development Plan will bring opportunity to review existing policy in line with the EPA policy. Cllr Keena requested the possibility of including this as an item for a future SPC meeting.

## Item 9: Date for Next Meeting {#item-9}

The next meeting is scheduled for Friday 14 November 2025 at 10am in Council Chambers. This concluded the business of the meeting.
