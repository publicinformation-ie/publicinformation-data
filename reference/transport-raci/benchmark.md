# Benchmark check

Benchmark run against `public/latest/transport-raci/llms.txt` and only the
files it links (README, METHODOLOGY, `transport-raci.json`, `raci-wide.csv`,
`rows.csv`, `assignments.csv`). No other source was used. The answers below are
the record required by the publication brief.

| Q | Question | Answer from the published files | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | Is a local authority responsible for choosing bus routes? | Not as the decision-maker. For BusConnects, `RT-17.S1` gives the National Transport Authority Accountable and Responsible, with the council executive and the Department of Transport Consulted (`not yet confirmed`). For TFI Local Link route design, `RT-19.S2` gives the NTA Accountable and Responsible, with the council executive, elected members and operators Consulted (`not yet confirmed`). | Answered, but the evidence is not yet confirmed. |
| b | Is a local authority responsible for maintaining active travel infrastructure (paths, cycle lanes)? | Yes. `RT-06.S12`, `RT-07.S12` and `RT-09.S12` give the council's Chief Executive and executive Accountable and Responsible, with delivery agents also Responsible and the elected members Informed. Evidence: `set by law` (s.13 Roads Act 1993). | High; set by law. |
| c | Is a local authority responsible for reducing traffic congestion? | Not yet assessed. No single activity owns "congestion"; the related activities (parking, traffic calming, network planning, traffic control, low-emission zones and land-use planning) are mostly not assessed. The data does not support a yes/no answer. | Not yet assessed. |
| d | Who is responsible for amending public transport fares? | Only Local Link fares are assessed: `RT-18.S13a` gives the National Transport Authority Accountable and Responsible, with Transport Co-ordination Units Consulted (`not yet confirmed`). Fares on other services are not assessed. | Partly answered; not yet assessed for non-Local Link fares. |
| e | Who is responsible for maintaining greenways? | `RT-10.S12` gives the council's Chief Executive and executive Accountable and Responsible, with TII Informed, but the evidence is `not yet confirmed` ("LA as asset owner by elimination"). On the Royal Canal Greenway (Waterways Ireland land) the Accountable party is not confirmed: the line records that it may be the council executive or Waterways Ireland, and the Meath section maintainer is unknown. | Answered with caveats; not confirmed. |
| f | Who is responsible for public train timetables? | Not yet assessed. `RL-03` (railway passenger and freight services) is entirely not assessed; no role is invented. | Not yet assessed. |

Coverage statement used: 613 rows, 184 assessed, 429 not assessed. The README
lists every role still awaiting review.
