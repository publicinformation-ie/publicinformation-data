## 1.0 Introduction to the New Transportation and Urban Regeneration SPC {#item-1-0}

## 3.0 Agree Work Programme / Agenda Items for next meeting {#item-3-0}

A possible work programme was circulated vis email prior to the Meeting. CC advised that issues which arise over the lifetime of the Committee, such as the EV charging Strategy for the County, The new speed limit review for urban areas will be brought to the SPC as a matter of course. A list of Objectives from the Development Plan, a list of policies, plans strategies and procedures that influence the delivery of work of the Transportation Dept and Chapter 2 of the National Sustainable Mobility Policy were circulated to those present and will be forwarded via email to all members. PM would like the Winter Gritting Programme added to the agenda. He wants a discussion to assess the challenges around how additional roads can be added to the programme. CC explained that there are a number of factors to consider for any expansion, such as the procurement of plant and machinery (ensuring any plant is fully utilised out of season), and the impact on day to day services, (ensuring winter maintenance personnel have sufficient rest period has a significant effect on the following day’s work.). She would arrange a presentation for the next SPC. 800Kms of Roads are salted during bad weather, sometime twice in a night, the programme is at capacity at the moment. PM said that there is an issue in his constituency where Roads leading into the County from other Local Authorities are gritted and the Roads on the Meath side of the border are not. AS said that they have the same issue in her MD. She considers this is very dangerous to come from a gritted road at speed onto a non-gritted road. SD wanted Road Safety added to the Work Schedule. He found that suggested traffic calming measures outside schools and public facilities were not being progressed as the MD were waiting for public realm plans to be adopted. Road safety should not be held up in this manner. SD wants to see that this SPC can make a difference and what Traffic Calming measures are progressed by the end of the term. SD also wanted clarity on the division of the funding for the Roadworks Programme. He does not feel that the funding is fairly divided and would like priority to be given to roads in the worst condition. He also asked for a presentation on Urban Regeneration to understand what project are ongoing. DA asked that the winter gritting Programme include cycleways and footpaths. COR agreed with SD and wanted an update on traffic calming measures in Batterstown and that lack of a footpath from the bus stop to Avoca in particular. CC advised the meeting that MCC Transportation Section get a huge amount of Reps in relation to traffic calming and hope to deal with these issues through a traffic calming policy. NW advised that active travel infrastructure is included in the Winter Gritting Programme. MB asked that public lighting be on the agenda. He would like to see some smaller and rural schemes progressed. He would like some clarity on the plan for Public lighting and footpaths as they are no longer funded under Clár or Town \& Village Funding. NW advised that funding comes from the NTA and more high profile cases tend to take precedence. It may be possible to develop criteria for the ranking of projects for agreement with the SPC. DA said he would like increased communication with the Public around active travel and that other Local Authorities for example Dublin City Council do this very well. An update on Navan Rail Line \& Public Transport – reliability and what the issues are how they can be addresses. In particular people being left behind. NW advised that the SPC can invite public bodies to come and make a presentation. A discussion was held around thew roadworks programme, road safety in general and the new speed limits.
