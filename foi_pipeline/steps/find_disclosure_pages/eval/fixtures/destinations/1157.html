Online Catalogue

Search the online library catalogue, order items and view your account.

A number of tasks can be completed using the online library system:

  • Browse Ireland’s online library catalogue for books, audiobooks, CDs, DVDs, and other material.
  • Order items from almost any library in Ireland to be delivered to your branch of choice.
  • Log in to your account to view what you have on loan, change your PIN, view your reading history, renew your items (if applicable) or subscribe to email or text alerts.
