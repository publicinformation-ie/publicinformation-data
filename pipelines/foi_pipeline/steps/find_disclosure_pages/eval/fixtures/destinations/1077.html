Disclosure Log

The Freedom of Information Act 2014 requires Freedom of Information (FOI) bodies such as the National Milk Agency to publish a disclosure log, which contains details of the types of requests received under FOI since January 2015 and the decisions made by the body in response to those requests.

Disclosures are listed in order of the date the request was received by the NMA. Please note that, for privacy reasons, identifying information such as the name of the requester is not included in the disclosure log.
