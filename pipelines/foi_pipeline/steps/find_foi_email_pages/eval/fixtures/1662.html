Freedom of Information Publication Scheme

Summary

What is the FOI Publication Scheme?

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2014, all FOI bodies must publish a scheme outlining how they share information with the public. This is known as the Freedom of Information Publication Scheme.

The Residential Tenancies Board (RTB) follows this requirement by providing access to key information through our FOI Publication Scheme, promoting openness, transparency, and accountability.

Our FOI Publication Scheme complies with the model publication scheme and guidelines issued by the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.

If you need more information

If you need information that cannot be found in this section, you can:
Call us on 0818 30 30 37 or 01 702 8100 (between 9am and 5pm, Monday to Friday) or
Email us at customer.service@rtb.ie
