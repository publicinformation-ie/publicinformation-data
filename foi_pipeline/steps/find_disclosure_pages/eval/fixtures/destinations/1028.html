HIGHLIGHTS

Statement of Strategy 2026-2030

The strategy provides a roadmap for the development of the Office over the coming years, for the benefit of Irish citizens, members of the Oireachtas, the bodies we audit and our staff.

Special Report 120 - NAMA: Progress on achievement of objectives as at end 2024

Section 226 of the National Asset Management Agency Act 2009 requires the Comptroller and Auditor General to assess, every three years, the extent to which NAMA has made progress toward achieving its overall objectives. This (fifth) review assesses NAMA’s progress on the achievement of its objectives up to the end of 2024.

Special Report 119 - Brexit physical infrastructure delivery and use

This report examines the use of public resources by public bodies in the delivery of the Brexit infrastructure programme and how the infrastructure delivered is being used.

17th annual INTOSAI Performance Audit Sub-committee meeting

The State Audit Office of Hungary hosted the 17th PAS annual two-day meeting in Budapest, Hungary on 24 – 25 March 2026. The meeting was chaired by the Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General.
