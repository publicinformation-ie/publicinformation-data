Special EU Programmes Body (SEUPB)

The Special EU Programmes Body (SEUPB) has the statutory remit for managing EU funding programmes within Northern Ireland and the border counties of Ireland helping to foster peace and prosperity across the region.

Change Maker Funding Programme

The PEACEPLUS Change Maker Funding Programme is open for applications. 

You can submit an application at any time between now and the 30th June 2026.  We encourage applicants to submit your application as soon as you are ready to do so, as an earlier submission will mean an earlier decision.  

The Change Maker Funding Programme offers a streamlined application process that allows community and voluntary groups, as well as other organisations, can access funding without being overwhelmed by administrative requirements. 

A total of €40 million will be invested directly into communities through grants of up to €100,000. These grants will support people-to-people projects that strengthen cross-community and cross-border relationships, helping to build peace and cohesion where it matters most.

Find out more about the Change Maker Funding Programme

PEACEPLUS

Calls are currently being accepted through the PEACEPLUS Programme

PEACEPLUS is a cross-border funding Programme supported by the European Union, the Government of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, the Government of Ireland, and the Northern Ireland administration. The Programme is designed to support peace and prosperity across Northern Ireland and the border counties of Ireland, building upon the work of the previous PEACE and INTERREG Programmes.

The Story and Impact of EU PEACE and INTERREG Funding

It has been more than 25 years since the establishment of the SEUPB and 2023 marked a significant milestone with the 25th anniversary of the Good Friday Agreement from which the SEUPB was created. This Impact Report was commissioned to chart the legacy of the four PEACE programmes, as well as the INTERREG A and Transnational Programmes.
