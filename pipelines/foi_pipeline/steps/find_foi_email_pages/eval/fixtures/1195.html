Freedom of Information

The Freedom of Information Act 2014 was enacted on 14th October 2014 and repeals the 1997 and 2003 Acts.

The information you seek may already be available elsewhere on this site which is intended to give ready access to information on current developments, to documentation of the Irish Expert Body on Fluorides and Health, and to its structure and functions. Before making any formal request under FOI, check to see if the information is already published or may be routinely available.

Should you wish to make an FOI request please contact the Dental Health Foundation by phone at 01-2136112, by email to foi@dentalhealth.ie or by post to:

FOI Section
Dental Health Foundation
PO Box 12343
Dublin 18

Publication Scheme

The Dental Health Foundation (DHF) has prepared a Publication Scheme in relation to information held by the DHF in line with the Model Publication Scheme and Guidelines provided by the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform under Section 8 of the Freedom of Information Act 2014.
